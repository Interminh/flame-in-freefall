## Summary

ACME s-Flame studied spherical diffusion flames in microgravity on the International Space Station. Gas fuel and diluent mixtures were fed through a spherical porous burner into an oxidizing environment: hydrogen, methane and diluent mixtures for flames without soot, and ethylene and diluent mixtures for sooty flames. Measurements included spectrally resolved images, flame and burner temperatures and radiative flux, compared with detailed simulations. Goals were transient flame structure, extinction limits at low and high Damköhler numbers, and pulsating instabilities. The record text is written in proposal form and gives no numerical results. Interpretation: fundamental flame physics and model validation, not material fire safety.

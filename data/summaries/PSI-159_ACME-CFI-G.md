## Summary

ACME CFI-G studied cool diffusion flames, which burn at low temperature, using gas fuels on spherical porous burners in the Combustion Integrated Rack on the International Space Station. Planned fuels were propane, n-butane and n-pentane, with normal and inverse flames and many reactant dilutions, flow rates and pressures. Cameras, radiometers, photomultiplier tubes and thermocouples observed the flames, and CFD and kinetics modeling support the tests. The hypotheses are that steady cool flames can be shown for the first time, with extinction when flow drops and a transition to hot flames when it rises. The record gives no numerical results. Interpretation: flame chemistry, not material fire safety.

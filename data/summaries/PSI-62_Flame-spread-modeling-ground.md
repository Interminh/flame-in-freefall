## Summary

This modeling study built and tested a flamelet generated manifold method, a way of simplifying flame chemistry in computer simulations, for flames spreading in the same direction as the airflow in microgravity. It was tested on BASS geometries: flat sheets, spheres, PMMA rods and wax cylinders. Images from the BASS and BASS-II experiments were processed with new two-color tomography using DSLR cameras to reconstruct soot and temperature fields for comparison. Model checks included flame geometry, temperature, soot volume fraction and flame spread rate, with focus on the 'Goldilocks' flammability zone. The stated aim is a screening tool for material flammability limits. The record gives no numerical results. Interpretation: a model built on BASS data, not new experiments.

## Summary

BASS ran fire tests on solid materials aboard the International Space Station in the Microgravity Science Glovebox. It had two goals: compare normal-gravity material screening with real microgravity burning, including flames behind obstacles, and test how well an inert gas extinguishing agent puts out flames. Samples included a cotton and fiberglass fabric (75% and 25% by mass), Nomex and Ultem, plus spheres and cylinders. Air flowed along the samples at up to 40 cm/s, with or against flame spread. Flame size, shape, color, spread rate and possible quenching were recorded. The record text states no numerical results. Interpretation: useful for fabric flammability and extinguishing questions.

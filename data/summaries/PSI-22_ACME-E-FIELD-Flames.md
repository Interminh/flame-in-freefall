## Summary

ACME E-FIELD Flames examined how electric fields change laminar gas-fuel diffusion flames in microgravity on the International Space Station, where buoyancy does not mask the effect. A voltage of up to 10 kV was applied between the burner and a mesh above it, with and without a weak air coflow. The team measured ion current through the flame and how fast the flame responds, and studied soot and stabilized near-limit lifted flames. The hypothesis is that electric fields can control flame stability and soot. The record says the work is not meant for space applications and gives no numerical results. Interpretation: little direct fire-safety relevance.

## Summary

SAFFIRE-I burned a large fabric sample, about 0.94 m by 0.4 m, inside an enclosure on a Cygnus cargo vehicle after it left the International Space Station. The sample was Sibal, 75% cotton and 25% fiberglass by weight. Air flowed through a duct at 20 cm/s. Two cameras, thermocouples, radiometers, and sensors for pressure, carbon dioxide, oxygen and flow recorded the burn. The goals were to learn how fast a large fire grows in low gravity and to compare low-gravity flammability limits with NASA's normal-gravity screening test. The record text states no numerical results. Interpretation: large-scale fire growth evidence for one fabric at one flow speed.

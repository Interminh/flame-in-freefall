## Summary

BASS-II tested how solid materials burn in microgravity aboard the International Space Station. Samples in rod, spherical, slab, film and fabric-sheet shapes sat in a small wind tunnel inside the Microgravity Science Glovebox, with airflow up to 40 cm/s either with or against flame spread. Oxygen was lowered below 21% by adding nitrogen. The team tracked ignition, flame growth, spread rate and extinction limits. It tests the hypothesis that materials burn as well as or better than in normal gravity under the same conditions. The record text states no numerical results. Interpretation: a good source for airflow and oxygen effects on solids, with numbers coming from the data files.

## Summary

SAFFIRE-II followed SAFFIRE-I on a Cygnus cargo vehicle that had left the International Space Station. A sample card held nine samples, each 5 cm by 29 cm, made from Sibal, silicone, structural PMMA, flat PMMA, and PMMA with Nomex. Depending on the material, air flowed at 20 or 30 cm/s, with or against flame spread. Cameras, thermocouples, radiometers and sensors for pressure, carbon dioxide, oxygen and flow recorded each burn. The goals match SAFFIRE-I: how fast a large fire grows and how low-gravity flammability limits compare with NASA's normal-gravity screening test. The record text states no numerical results. Interpretation: adds material variety to the SAFFIRE series.

## Summary

ACME Flame Design studied spherical diffusion flames of ethylene and methane, in normal and inverse form, in drop towers and on the International Space Station. The team varied flow rate, Zst, nitrogen or carbon dioxide dilution and pressure, observing with cameras, radiometers, photomultiplier tubes and thermocouples. Goals were soot-inception limits, extinction limits, and flame temperature, size, color and soot volume. Hypotheses: soot needs a minimum carbon-to-oxygen ratio and temperature, microgravity spherical flames extinguish when peak temperature falls below 1130 K, and steady spherical flames are possible. The record gives no numerical results. Interpretation: soot and extinction limits of gas flames, not solid-material flammability.

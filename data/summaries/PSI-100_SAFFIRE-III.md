## Summary

SAFFIRE-III followed SAFFIRE-I on a Cygnus cargo vehicle after it left the International Space Station, and also burned Sibal fabric, a cotton and fiberglass composite. The change was a faster air flow of 30 cm/s instead of 20 cm/s, with the flow either with or against flame spread. Cameras, thermocouples, radiometers and pressure, carbon dioxide, oxygen and flow sensors recorded the burns, and the data were compressed and downlinked. Goals were fire growth rate and low-gravity flammability limits compared with NASA's normal-gravity screening test. The record text states no numerical results. Interpretation: a flow-speed comparison against SAFFIRE-I for a single material.

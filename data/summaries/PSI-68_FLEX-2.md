## Summary

FLEX-2 extended FLEX to fuels and conditions closer to real combustors, using the Multi-user Droplet Combustion Apparatus on the International Space Station. Chamber pressure ranged from 0.5 to 3.0 atm and oxygen from 0.1 to 0.4 mole fraction, with a diluent gas as the balance. Droplets were placed on a support fiber or freely deployed, and could be moved to create a flow. Goals included how liquid-phase mixing affects bi-component droplets, extinction mechanisms, soot formation and droplet arrays, all to benchmark numerical models. The record text states no numerical results. Interpretation: useful for droplet extinction and pressure and oxygen effects, with little direct link to solid-material fires.

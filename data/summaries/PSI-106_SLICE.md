## Summary

SLICE studied the structure and stability of laminar gas diffusion flames in microgravity on the International Space Station. A fan-driven flow duct with exchangeable burner tubes and sensors was used with methane, ethylene and nitrogen-diluted mixtures, ignited manually in a controlled coflow of air. More than 4,000 high-resolution images were taken across 13 experiments. Goals included liftoff velocity limits for different fuels and burner sizes, the effect of nitrogen dilution on soot and stability, flame length and radiative heat loss, and validating models. The hypothesis is that burner diameter, coflow velocity and fuel composition strongly affect these flames. The record gives no numerical results. Interpretation: flame stability data with no solid fuels.
